🚨 6 Critical Red Flags to Watch Out For
⚡ 1. Artificial Urgency & Threats
"Account deleted in 24 hours!" or "Final notice: tuition overdue!" Attackers manufacture fear so you react before verifying.
🌐 2. Lookalike Subdomains
Domains like "campus.edu.fake-portal.test" are NOT your university. The true root domain is always right before the final extension.
🔑 3. Requests for Passwords or MFA
Legitimate IT departments will NEVER ask for your password or prompt you to speak back 6-digit MFA one-time passcodes.
💼 4. Remote Job & Advance-Fee Scams
$35/hr student jobs with no interview and an upfront "equipment check" are 100% fake-check overpayment fraud.
📱 5. Delivery SMS (Smishing) Links
Text messages claiming failed package delivery demanding a $1.99 fee with shortened links (bit.ly) exist only to harvest credit cards.
📎 6. Dangerous Double Extensions
Files disguised as "Grade_Report.pdf.exe" or documents prompting you to "Enable Macros" install hidden malware.

🛡️ Routine Safe Habits

  • Bookmark your university Single Sign-On (SSO) login.
  • Enable phishing-resistant Multi-Factor Authentication.
  • Use an authentic password manager to prevent autofill on fake domains.
  • Report suspicious messages using your email client's "Report Phishing" button.

⚠️ If You Already Clicked or Entered Info

  • Immediately change your university password from a different device.
  • Revoke active login sessions and reset MFA authentication tokens.
  • If financial info was entered, notify your bank fraud department.
  • Contact Campus IT Helpdesk immediately so they can protect other accounts.
